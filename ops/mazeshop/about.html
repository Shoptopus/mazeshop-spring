HELLO FROM CHEPSTOW

A little workshop.
A lot of ideas.

We’re Agnes, Roland and family: the people behind Mazeshop. We make personalised gifts, wooden puzzles and decorative pieces that bring a little more character to everyday life.

HOW IT STARTED

Design has always
been part of the story.

Roland’s background includes furniture design and CAD, followed by a move into software engineering. The enjoyment of making a physical thing never quite went away.

When our family moved to Chepstow in 2024, a garage became the start of a workshop. A laser cutter followed, along with plenty of experiments, learning and ideas shared around the family.

Today, that workshop is where we develop our gifts, puzzles, engraved pieces and home décor. Agnes helps shape the creative direction, and making things remains very much a family effort.
