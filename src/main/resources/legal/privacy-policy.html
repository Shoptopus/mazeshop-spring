Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This notice explains how we handle personal information when you use the Mazeshop online shop, place an order or contact us. It also explains the Google connection used to send shop emails.

Who we are · Information we use · Google and Gmail · Your rights

What we collect and why

  • Orders and enquiries: your name, email, telephone number if supplied, billing and delivery details, purchases, personalisation instructions, uploaded artwork and correspondence. We use these to answer requests, make and deliver products, provide support and handle returns. Our lawful basis is taking steps at your request before a contract and performing our contract with you.
  • Accounts: contact details, a protected password representation, account preferences and order history. We use these to provide your requested account and keep it secure, relying on our contract with you and our legitimate interests in preventing unauthorised access.
  • Payment and accounting records: order totals, payment status and payment-provider references. Where online payment is available, the checkout identifies the provider handling your payment details. We use payment records to fulfil the order and meet legal accounting and tax obligations.
  • Technical and security information: IP addresses, request and error logs, authentication events and changes to shop records. Our legitimate interests are operating a reliable service, diagnosing faults and preventing misuse or fraud.

We usually receive information directly from you, from your use of the shop or from a payment or delivery provider involved in your order. You may also give us a recipient's delivery details for a gift. Please only supply information and artwork you are entitled to share. If you do not provide details needed for an order, we may be unable to fulfil it.

Who receives information?

Access is limited to people who need it to operate the business and provide your order or support. We share relevant information with delivery companies, payment providers when used, professional advisers and authorities where legally required. We use Google Gmail to deliver shop messages and Amazon Web Services for encrypted backups. We do not sell personal information.

Messages may include your email address, name, order details and the content needed to answer your enquiry. Service providers receive the information needed for their role, under their applicable terms and privacy notices.

Google account connection and Gmail

The shop administrator can connect the shop's Google account so Mazeshop can send transactional messages, such as order updates and password-reset emails. Customers do not need to connect a Google account or grant Gmail access to buy from us.

The integration requests permission to send email through Gmail and basic account identity to identify the connected sender. It does not request permission to read the Gmail inbox. We receive the connected account's email address and authorisation tokens, store them in restricted server settings, and use them to send and authenticate shop email. Tokens are also included in encrypted operational backups.

We do not sell Google account data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Mazeshop's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

The administrator can disconnect Gmail in shop settings or revoke access in Google Account connections. Disconnecting removes the active stored authorisation tokens; revoking access at Google invalidates the grant. You can contact us to request deletion of associated information. Historic messages and legally required business records may remain, and backup copies expire through the backup cycle described below.

Storage, security and international transfers

The shop uses HTTPS, restricted administrative access and encrypted backups. Backups are stored in the AWS London region. Google operates internationally, so email and connected-account information may be processed outside the UK. Google's published data-transfer safeguards include the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses where applicable. Contact us for further information about transfers relevant to your data.

How long we keep information

We keep account and order information while needed to provide the service, deal with enquiries or disputes, and meet legal obligations. Company accounting records are normally kept for six years from the end of the financial year they relate to, and longer where the law requires. Other correspondence, uploaded files and security records are retained according to their purpose, whether an issue remains open and any continuing legal need; we review information and remove or anonymise it when no longer needed.

Backups follow a rolling cycle of seven daily, four weekly and three monthly snapshots. Removed versions in offsite storage can remain for up to a further 30 days. A deletion from the live shop may therefore remain in protected backups until those copies rotate out.

Cookies, browser storage and marketing

The shop uses browser storage and essential cookies for functions such as signing in, remembering a basket and maintaining secure access. During the preview period, an access cookie remembers a successful preview login. You can clear or block cookies and browser storage in your browser; doing so may affect these functions.

This privacy page does not load analytics, advertising scripts or external fonts. We send service messages needed for your account or order. If we introduce optional marketing or non-essential tracking, we will explain it and obtain consent where required. You can ask us to stop direct marketing at any time.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask to access or correct your information, erase it, restrict its use, receive a portable copy, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful use. Some information may need to be retained to meet legal obligations or establish or defend claims.

Email mazeshopsales@gmail.com to make a request. We may need to verify your identity. We normally respond within one month; the law allows additional time in certain circumstances, which we will explain.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK data-protection regulator. We would welcome the opportunity to resolve your concern first.

Changes to this notice

We will update this page when our practices change, show the new date above and provide further notice where required. Please contact us if anything here is unclear.
